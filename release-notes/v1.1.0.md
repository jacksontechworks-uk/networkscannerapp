## JacksonTechWorks Network Scanner v1.1.0

**Already running v1.0.0?** You don't need to download anything. Open the app and an "update available" banner appears. Click **Update now** and it downloads, verifies and installs this version, then restarts.

### 🆕 New in this release
- **⚙ Settings menu** in the header, with **DNS**, **General** and **Updates** pages.
- **DNS switcher**: change any adapter's DNS to **Automatic (DHCP)**, **Quad9**, Quad9 unfiltered, **Cloudflare** (standard, malware blocking, or malware + adult blocking), **Google**, **OpenDNS**, **AdGuard**, or your own custom servers. Covers IPv4 and, optionally, IPv6. Shows a UAC prompt and flushes the DNS cache afterwards.
- **DNS benchmark** to find the fastest resolver from your location, plus a **Flush DNS cache** button.
- **General settings**: SSH username, ping and port timeouts, speed-test port and default ports.
- **Updates**: the app also checks every 6 hours while it's open. There's a toggle, *Check now*, and the time of the last check.

### ✅ Still working from v1.0.0
Subnet scan (ICMP + ARP), port scan, MAC vendors, filters, right-click launchers (web, SSH, RDP, SMB…), Live Ping with route and world map, Traceroute, PathPing, LAN speed test, Wake-on-LAN, PDF/CSV export and self-update.

### 🚧 Up & coming
Scan profiles, NetBIOS/mDNS discovery, service banners, SNMP, scheduled scans with new-device alerts, device history, ping alerts, multi-stream and UDP speed tests, DNS-over-HTTPS setup, IPv6 scanning, and code-signed builds.
